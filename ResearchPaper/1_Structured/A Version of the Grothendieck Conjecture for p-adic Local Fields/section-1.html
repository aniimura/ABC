Section 1: The Cyclotomic Character and Inertia

原文 p.2

Fix a prime number p. Let K be a p-adic local field, meaning a finite extension of Qp. Fix an algebraic closure K̄ of K. Let ΓK := Gal(K̄/K). The purpose of this paper is to examine to what extent K can be recovered “group-theoretically” from ΓK — meaning: given another p-adic local field K′ and an isomorphism of topological groups α : ΓK ≅ ΓK′, the object associated to K is necessarily taken by α to the corresponding object associated to K′.

【構造化メモ】この節の結果は既知(著者自身がSection末尾のRemarkで明記)——本論文の新規性は§4(主定理)にあり、§1は準備。ただし「group-theoretically」という中心概念の**定義**(本段落)自体は精密な形式化を要する: 「∀K′, ∀α:Γ_K≅Γ_K′(位相群として), α(Xの対象)=X′の対応する対象」という**普遍的な**(全てのK′・αについての)条件——これをLean化する際は`∀`の範囲(K′は同じpの局所体全体を渡る)を正確に書く必要がある。

原文 p.2-3

We begin by reviewing duality. Suppose M is a Zp-module of finite length equipped with a continuous ΓK-action. Then (see [2], Proposition 3.8) one has a natural isomorphism of Galois cohomology modules $$H^i(K, M) \cong H^{2-i}(K, M^\vee(1))^\vee \quad (i \geq 0)$$ where “(1)” is a Tate twist and “∨” denotes the Pontrjagin dual (Hom(−, Qp/Zp)). Suppose M ≅ Z/pnZ as a Zp-module. Then M ≅ Z/pnZ(1) as a ΓK-module if and only if H2(K, M) ≅ Z/pnZ. This is a group-theoretic condition on M. Thus the isomorphism class of the ΓK-module Zp(1) can be recovered group-theoretically from ΓK.

【構造化メモ、境界外候補①】ここで引用される「local Tate duality」([2], Bloch-Kato Prop.3.8)がProposition1.1の**証明の核心的な入力**——mathlib実測調査でGalois cohomology・local duality系は不在と確認済み。境界外公理化の第一候補。

原文 p.3

Proposition 1.1: The cyclotomic character χ : ΓK → Zp× can be recovered entirely group-theoretically from ΓK.

証明: 直前のduality review段落そのものが証明の全内容(円分指標のグラフ = Z_p(1)の同型類のデータそのもの、という直接的な帰結)。

原文 p.3

Now recall from local class field theory (see, e.g., [3]) that we have a natural isomorphism ΓKab ≅ (K×)∧ (profinite completion). Let k be the residue field of OK, so k has q = pf elements. It is well-known that (K×)∧ fits into an exact sequence of topological groups $$0 \to U_K \to (K^\times)^\wedge \to \mathbf{Z} \to 0$$ where UK := OK×. In particular, the prime-to-p part of the torsion subgroup of (K×)∧ has precisely q−1 elements, and the pro-p quotient of (K×)∧ is the direct sum of a torsion group and a free Zp-module of rank [K:Qp]+1.

【構造化メモ、境界外候補②】この段落の「local class field theory」([3], Serre)への言及は、mathlib実測調査で不在と確認済み(局所類体論の相互写像そのもの)。Proposition1.2の証明の核心的な入力——境界外公理化の第二候補。「exact sequence 0→U_K→(K×)^→Z→0」自体は(局所CFTを経由せず)直接K×の構造から従う初等的事実である可能性があり、この部分は境界内で構築できるか要検討。

原文 p.3

Proposition 1.2: The number q of elements in the residue field of OK, as well as the absolute degree [K:Qp] of K, can be recovered entirely group-theoretically from ΓK.

原文 p.3

Now let H ⊆ ΓK be an open subgroup, and let L ⊇ K be the corresponding extension field. Applying Proposition 1.2 to L and H, the number qL of elements in the residue field of OL can be recovered group-theoretically from H ⊆ ΓK. Note that L is unramified over K if and only if qL = q[ΓK:H].

原文 p.3

Corollary 1.3: The inertia subgroup IK ⊆ ΓK can be determined group-theoretically from ΓK.

【構造化メモ】"determined group-theoretically"の内実: I_K = {開部分群Hの共通部分 | Hに対応する拡大体Lが不分岐} という**特徴づけ**として読める——「不分岐⟺q_L=q^[Γ_K:H]」という判定条件(Prop1.2から従う)を使い、I_Kを「全ての不分岐拡大に対応する開部分群の共通部分」として復元する、という構成的な議論だと解釈できる。原文はこの最終ステップ(共通部分を取る)を明示的には書いていない——①省略補完の対象。

原文 p.3-4

Remark: It follows, in particular, that (i) [K:Qp] and (ii) the maximal abelian (over Qp) subfield of K are determined group-theoretically by ΓK. The content of Propositions 1.1 and 1.2 is essentially contained in [8], and Corollary 1.3 is explicitly stated in §1 of [9].
