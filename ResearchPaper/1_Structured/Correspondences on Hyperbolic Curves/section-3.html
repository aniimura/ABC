Definition 3.1.
Suppose that X is not arithmetic. Then we shall refer to Y (respectively,
Y) as the hyperbolic core of X (respectively, X ).
Proposition 3.2.
We have ΓZ ⊆Comm(ΓX ).
Theorem 3.3.
Suppose that X is not arithmetic. Fix a pair of nonnegative integers
(g′, r′) such that 2g′ −2 + r′ > 0. Then there exist (up to isomorphism) only finitely many
hyperbolic curves Z of type (g′, r′) that are isogenous to Z.
