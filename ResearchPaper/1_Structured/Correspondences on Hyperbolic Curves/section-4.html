Lemma 4.1.
Suppose that XK is isogenous to some hyperbolic curve ZK over K.
Then ZK is the result of base-extending some hyperbolic curve Z over k from k to K, and,
moreover, any correspondence from XK to ZK descends to a correspondence from X to Z.
